About

What Is CLRD?

A call log and SMS cleaner for Android. No blocker, no cloud, no nonsense — just clean.

The Problem

Your Call Log Is a Mess

Spam calls don't just ring you — they leave a trail. Every rejected call, every screened robocall, every zero-second "Spam Risk" entry stays in your call log forever unless you delete it manually, one at a time. Nobody does that.

CLRD does it for you.

The App

What CLRD Does

CLRD scans your call log on launch, pre-selects the junk, and lets you nuke it in one tap. You can review before you delete, deselect anything you want to keep, or flip on Auto-Clean and never think about it again.

The SMS scanner works the same way — CLRD shows you your message threads, you mark the ones you want gone, and it clears them. You can even send a STOP reply before deleting if you want to opt out of a sender's list.

One Tap
Spam pre-selected. Tap NUKE. Done. No menus, no settings maze.
Auto-Clean
Toggle it on and CLRD silently clears spam every time you open it.
Blocklist
Add specific numbers and CLRD wipes their entries silently on every launch.
Ghost Mode
Premium. Same as the Blocklist but for contacts you never want to see in your call history at all.
SMS Cleanup
Scan, review, and delete SMS/MMS threads. Send STOP replies before clearing.
Fully Local
No network calls. Nothing leaves your device. No account, no cloud, no data sold.
Platform

Android Only (For Now)

CLRD is Android-only. iOS does not give third-party apps access to call history or SMS data — the hooks simply don't exist on Apple's platform. This isn't a compromise, it's just the reality of what Apple allows. CLRD exists because Android lets developers actually build things like this.

CLRD is available on the Google Play Store.

Pricing

Free to Use. One Optional Upgrade.

CLRD is free. The core call log cleaner, SMS scanner, Auto-Clean, Blocklist, and all filters are included at no cost.

Ghost Mode — which silently removes call log entries from specific numbers on every launch — is a one-time in-app purchase of $4.99, processed by Google Play. No subscription, no trial, no bullshit.

The Builder
